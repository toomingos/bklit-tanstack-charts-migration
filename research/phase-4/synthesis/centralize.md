# Phase 4.2.1.2 Synthesis — Centralize

Lens: centralize duplicated logic. Backbone: `research/phase-4/usage-matrix.md` (redo). Every proposed merge verified against source under `showcase/migrated/charts/` on 2026-08-21 (grep + direct reads; stale/nuanced matrix claims corrected inline below).

Main goal constraint: centralization must not change rendered design or interactivity. Where a merge risks behavioral divergence, it is marked and the divergent constant/branch is named. Fewer, larger consolidations preferred; micro-merges folded into family rows.

Legend: Target = proposed module. "Stays per-part" = what must NOT move. Risk ratings assume gates Q1/Q2/G1–G4 must stay green.

5. Reveal-guard standardization: should 4.3 converge all inline `bkmRevealed` stamping onto `deferred-reveal.checkRevealGuard`, and does the svg-root variant (ring/radar/sunburst/choropleth) warrant its own shared helper given marks-group vs svg-root targets differ?

7. Gauge center intro: the double-rAF 0→value trick is now the sole copy of legacy `PieCenterShell` logic — promote into `CenterShell` as opt-in, or leave local to gauge-center?
8. Row 2 (chrome skeleton) and row 3 (spring runtime factory): pursue only if 4.3 capacity allows after rows 1/4/6/13; both are the highest-risk merges for near-zero LOC gain.
