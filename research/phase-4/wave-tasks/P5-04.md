Recommendation, not a mandate: option 1 is almost certainly right for Wave 5 — restoring the *reachable* default export (the one thing you'd actually `import Foo from '.../foo-chart'` for) satisfies the practical parity goal without an unscoped refactor; the sub-components were never meaningfully imported by their default in isolation by real callers.
