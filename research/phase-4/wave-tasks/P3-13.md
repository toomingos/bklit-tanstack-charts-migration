## Row 27 — Radar long-format reshape → `fold`

`radar-chart.tsx:50` — `Z_PAD = 5`; consumed at `:304` — `series: String(i).padStart(Z_PAD, "0")` inside the `allRows` reshape (`RadarRow` interface at `:196-200`: `{metric, value, series}`), building a long-format row list from radar's wide per-area data. Native target: `fold` (`@tanstack/charts/transform/fold`).

Per tanstack.md's own gap note: "series-id Z-pad stays custom." The zero-padded `series` string exists purely to force **stable lexicographic sort order** across more than 9 series (e.g. `"00"`, `"01"`, ..., `"10"` sorts correctly where `"0"`,`"1"`,...,`"10"` would not) — this is a presentation/sort-stability concern adjacent to but distinct from the reshape itself. If `fold` performs the wide-to-long reshape natively, `Z_PAD`'s zero-padding logic still needs to be applied to whatever series identifier `fold` produces (or applied before/after it) — it does not disappear just because the reshape moves to a native transform.

### Gate
