## Strand 3 — the ring hover defect (D258). READ `docs/phase-4/LOG.md` D258 BEFORE TOUCHING RING.

### Status change

D255 previously ruled ring **ACCEPT** (inherited legacy behavior, not migration drift). **That ruling is OVERTURNED by D258.** Ring is a genuine migration defect. Do not re-cite D255's ACCEPT.

D255's *structural* claim was correct and still holds — legacy does bind enter/leave to the same `motion.g` it springs (`repos/bklit-ui/packages/ui/src/charts/ring.tsx:177-220`, all three return branches). What was wrong was the inference that legacy therefore oscillates too. Measured: **it does not, at any density** (n=4/20/100/1000, `elementFromPoint` churn = 0 every time, byte-identical settled state across reps).

### The measured evidence

At the harness's own probe (svgBox 24,24,280x280 -> hover-30 = **(108,164)**, radius 56px from centre), sampling per animation frame for 2500ms:

| density | bklit churn | migrated churn | migrated settles? |
|---|---|---|---|
| n=4 | 0 | 0 | yes (but see gap below) |
| n=20 | 0 | 1 | yes |
| n=100 | 0 | 50-56 | no |
| n=1000 (gate density) | 0 | 28-32 | **no — 38 distinct transform signatures still cycling in the final second** |

At n=1000 the two reps freeze at *different* spring positions (`scale(1.00247)` vs `scale(1.00658)`) with the cursor ending over bare `<svg>`; bklit settles identically both reps (ring 999 @ `scale(1.03)`). This is the direct cause of D256's bimodal gate distribution — the t=700ms capture samples a random phase of a loop that has no steady state.

**Mechanism:** hover grows the band radially (`scale(1.03)` hovered, `1.02` outer neighbours). Once the band is thinner than the growth displacement (~1.7px at r=56 for 1.03), the hovered band grows out from under a *stationary* cursor -> `pointerleave` -> spring reverses -> band returns -> `pointerenter` -> sustained loop. bklit escapes because its hover latches a ring far from the cursor (ring 999 at r~129 while the cursor sits at r=56), so nothing it grows can dislodge it.

### The prescribed fix, and its limit

`research/phase-4/wave-tasks/F4-ring.md` prescribes binding the listeners to an **unscaled wrapper `<g>`** around track+progress — zero pixel change, kills the ejection. That is the right shape for the oscillation.

**But it does not address the second finding.** At **n=4** — the most realistic ring count, and a density where *neither* side oscillates — bklit shows the full push-out (`scale(1.03)` + 3x `scale(1.02)`) while **migrated ends completely flat, all groups at `scale(1)`**. Isolated with a three-way probe:

- bklit teleported straight to the probe (steps:1, crossing no band): **no emphasis**
- bklit swept in the harness's way (steps:10 from (2,2)): **emphasis**
- bklit then moved to an empty corner inside the svg: **releases**

So bklit acquires hover on the fly-in crossing and retains it while the cursor rests in the centre hole; migrated releases. At that probe an annulus sweep finds no ring geometry in *either* impl, so this is a latch/release divergence, not a hit-test hole. **This gap is open and unruled — decide it explicitly, do not let the wrapper fix paper over it.**

### Already ruled out — do NOT re-investigate

**Pivot semantics.** Both impls declare `transformOrigin: "0px 0px"`, and it is tempting to theorise that bklit's `transform-box: fill-box` vs migrated's `view-box` makes bklit translate on hover. **Measured and false.** Framer Motion overrides bklit's declaration to fill-box `50% 50%`, computing to `72/90/108/126px` per ring — each ring's own bbox centre, i.e. the shared ring centre. migrated satisfies `dx = -dw/2` and `dy = -dh/2` exactly on every row (ring-6: -1.72/3.45; ring-19: -2.64/5.28). **Both pivot about the ring centre; hover is pure radial growth with zero translation in both.** The comment block in `ring-chart.tsx` claiming the `0px 0px` pin achieves centre-pivot is right about the outcome even though its stated reasoning differs.

### Secondary finding to fix while you are here

`ring-chart.tsx` (hover-chrome `useLayoutEffect`, ~lines 624-796) stamps `style.cursor`, `style.transformOrigin`, and `style.pointerEvents = "none"` on the progress group and its path. **On the live DOM all three read empty**, while the listeners demonstrably still fire (a synthetic `pointerenter` at `ring-25-track` produces ring-25 `scale(1.03)` and outer rings `scale(1.02)`). TanStack is rewriting the style attribute after the wiring effect runs, so the `pointer-events:none` guard the code's own comment depends on ("the track remains the hit target everywhere") is **not holding in practice**. Either re-establish it in a way TanStack will not clobber, or remove the comment's false claim along with the dead stamps.
