## Metrics (research/phase-1/04-metrics-and-baselines.md)

### M1 — Time to render (initial mount)
- **M1a mount-to-paint**: `root.render()` → first paint after SVG commit.
- **M1b settle time**: mount → visually settled (`window.__benchSettled`).
- **M1c scripting cost of mount**: main-thread scripting during mount.

### M2 — Runtime load (steady state)
- **M2a idle CPU**: task time during 5s post-settle, no input.
- **M2b heap**: `usedJSHeapSize` after settle + forced GC.
- **M2c bundle cost**: min+gzip of scenario entry chunk.

### M3 — Onchange & interactivity
- **M3a data-update latency**: median/p95 update→paint over 30 swaps.
- **M3b sustained throughput**: FPS / dropped-frame % (live charts only).
- **M3c hover cost**: median/worst frame time during 60-step sweep.

## Benchmark gates (research/phase-1/05-qa-and-benchmark-gates.md)
