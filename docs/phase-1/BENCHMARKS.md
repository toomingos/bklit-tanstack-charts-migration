## Metrics (research/04)

### M1 — Time to render (initial mount)

- **M1a mount-to-paint**: time from `root.render()` to first paint after the chart's SVG is committed (excludes animation completion).
- **M1b settle time**: mount to chart visually settled (mount/reveal animations finished, via `window.__benchSettled`).
- **M1c scripting cost of mount**: total main-thread scripting time during the mount window.

### M2 — Runtime load (steady state)

- **M2a idle CPU**: main-thread task time during 5 s post-settle with no input (catches always-running RAF/animation loops).
- **M2b heap**: `usedJSHeapSize` after settle + forced GC.
- **M2c bundle cost**: min+gzip size of the scenario entry chunk attributable to the chart.

### M3 — Onchange & interactivity

- **M3a data-update latency**: median/p95 update→paint time over 30 seeded dataset swaps.
- **M3b sustained update throughput**: achieved FPS and dropped-frame % driving updates every frame for 5 s (live-data scenario).
- **M3c hover cost**: median/worst frame time during a scripted 60-step pointer sweep, with tooltip appearance asserted.
- **M3d brush/drag** (charts with brush only): frame-time distribution during a scripted drag across the track.

## Benchmark gates (research/05)

`B` = bklit baseline, `T` = native TanStack baseline (ceiling), `M` = migrated. Lower is better for all metrics except M3b FPS (higher is better, inverted in gate math).

- **G1 (improvement)**: `M` beats `B` on M1a, M1c, M2a, M3a, M3c, with ≥ 20 % improvement required on M1a, M3a, M3c (north stars); no metric may regress vs. `B` by more than 5 %.
- **G2 (closeness, when `T` exists)**: gap-closure ratio `(B − M) / (B − T) ≥ 0.6` on M1a, M3a, M3c (waived for charts with no native TanStack equivalent — see `docs/PROGRESS.md` gap rows; rationale logged in `docs/LOG.md`).
- **G3 (steady state)**: M2a idle CPU ≤ 50 % of `B`'s, and within 2× of `T`.
- **G4 (memory/bundle)**: M2b and M2c must not exceed `B` by more than 10 %.

A migration is approved only when QA (Q1/Q2, see `docs/PROGRESS.md`) and G1–G4 all pass, from scripts, on the same run.
