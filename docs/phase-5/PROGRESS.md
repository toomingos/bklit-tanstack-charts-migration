## Post-hoc: the "six consecutive no-op batches" narrative, revised (2026-09-01)

**Appended after Phase 5 closed. Nothing above is rewritten** — the record of what we believed, and
why, is the point of keeping it.

Phase 5 closed with six task-groups skipped as not-implementable (B1c, B1d, B2, B3, B4, B7) and
narrated that streak as six genuine dead ends against a library that could not do the work. A
re-audit against the post-C4 Phase-6 tree — `research/phase-6/09-reopened-phase-5.md`, ledger
**D434–D446** — shows the narrative is **partly a reconciliation failure, not six dead ends**:

- **Six tasks were ALREADY DONE, or became done within weeks, under different names.** T13 (B4) ships
  as Phase-6 C2. T12 and T9 (B3) ship as C3. T5 (B1c) ships as C4. T10 (B3) and T21a (B2) were met
  by ordinary app-side work nobody mapped back to the task row. In several cases the *same
  acceptance criteria* were satisfied by a commit landed after the skip was logged, and no one
  reconciled the two.
- **Three verdicts were factually wrong about the library, not about the code.** D401's subpath claim
  (T20), D404's mark-selection claim (T15), and D390's API selection (T21b) each audited the wrong
  symbol and generalized the result to "not implementable".
- **Four verdicts were correct at the time and expired.** D379, D381(T21a), D382 and D386 all rested
  on architecture that C1–C4 replaced. D417's standing rule — every "library can't do X" ruling
  carries a version stamp and expires at the next pin bump — exists precisely for this class and is
  what forced the re-audit.
- **Two verdicts stand as written.** T7's non-rect clip and T21b's binning half are genuinely absent
  at `@tanstack/charts@0.15.0`; D380's block on *deleting* the resize hook is upheld, though its
  acceptance criterion was unachievable as written and is restated at D444.

The process lesson is narrower than "the task rows were wrong" (which Phase 5 already logged eleven
times over). It is that **a skip is a claim with a shelf life, and nothing in the phase re-checked
one.** Two habits follow: a skipped task's acceptance criteria must be re-read whenever a later
commit touches the same subsystem, and a "not implementable" ruling must name the exact symbol it
audited — D401, D404 and D390 would each have been caught by the second habit alone.

None of this changes a Phase-5 gate result, a QA baseline, or a shipped line of code. It changes
the disposition of ten task rows, all of which are re-dispositioned in
`research/phase-5/go-to-plan.md` and scheduled on the Phase-6 ladder (C5a, C5b, C5c, C5d, 6.4).
